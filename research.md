# Research Interests

My research interests focus on Environmental assessment, WASH and Air Pollution.

## Environmental and Social Impact Assessment
- Environmental and socioeconomic impacts of transport infrastructure
- Environmental assessment practices and regulatory implementation
- Environmental considerations in infrastructure planning
- Assessment of environmental and social risks associated with development projects

## Water, Sanitation, and Hygiene (WASH)
- Evaluation of drinking water and sanitation services
- Water and sanitation conditions in urban areas
- Bangladesh's progress toward Sustainable Development Goal 6
- Assessment of water and sanitation service quality

## Air Pollution
- Assessment of ambient air quality and air pollution levels in urban areas
- Prediction and forecasting of PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ concentrations in Dhaka and other major cities in Bangladesh
- Spatiotemporal analysis of particulate matter concentrations and air pollution hotspots
- Evaluation of meteorological, traffic-related, industrial, and land-use factors influencing PM₂.₅ and PM₁₀ levels
- Application of machine learning and statistical models for air quality prediction and pollution trend analysis
