## About Me

My academic background combines civil engineering, environmental science and management, and ongoing postgraduate study in environmental engineering. My professional experience includes university teaching, environmental engineering consultancy, and research activities.

My research interests focus on Environmental and Social Impact Assessment practices in Bangladesh, Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH), gender in WASH, drinking water and sanitation services, and air pollution.
